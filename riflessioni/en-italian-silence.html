13 August 2021 · Written by Star Bstreet

Italian Silence


Keeping quiet makes us look peaceful and lets us avoid having enemies; raising white flags gives us the chance not to end up in pointless battles. After all, why bother? Right?
Then we complain, we cry, we curse those who govern us, because we realise that keeping quiet and siding with what we believe is the good and right majority is in reality only DISGUISED INJUSTICE that sooner or later will bear its fruit.

Free

PEOPLE are dying around us, and us? We move forward in the tunnel of silence, indifferent, because we are safe, we are alive, our families haven't been hit, we can go out, sit in restaurants, celebrate birthdays, hug, dance, toast and shout: "free at last!"
Yes! Free in the antechamber of hell!

Meanwhile families are mourning their loved ones who died because of a vaccine that is NOT a vaccine.
We only want to hear news that serves our interests, and whatever happens outside our "fences" hardly hurts us.

But mark this: there will be terrible times in the last days. People will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, proud, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,
without love, unforgiving, slanderous, without self-control, brutal,
not lovers of the good, treacherous, rash, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, having a form of godliness but denying its power.... (2 Tim 3:1...)

This is what is happening today!

Selfishness is advancing, and so is the NORMALITY of this whole scene.

Where are our values? Where is our FAITH?
PERHAPS it too has become so expensive that we prefer a HARD DISCOUNT , cutting costs with SILENCE?

After all, everyone who wants to remain faithful to God and united with Jesus Christ will be persecuted. (2 Tim 3:12)

My prayer

May our hearts be kept from every lie, so that we may still be free in the freedom Christ has given us, to proclaim His Truth and NoT ours.
