From dust to dust



"From dust to dust" actually came to mind because I was watching a video on YouTube and noticed some really annoying dust on the base of a lamp in that video, which was otherwise very interesting. My immediate reaction was to think about this YouTuber's lack of cleanliness.

In reality the annoyance was all mine. Mine, in seeing that dust. That annoyance was mine alone. The answer as to why came to me almost immediately, with an incredible sense of simplicity: you are dust, and to dust you shall return.

I won't hide that seeing that dust gave me a feeling of disgust, just as, humanly, you can feel disgust at seeing an attitude you don't like, even from someone you don't know.

We are so caught up in what we look at that we lose the meaning of our life. We focus on trivial things that lead us only to waste time. Wait a moment, though, maybe it's only my problem; I should stop writing that it's our problem, I think it's only mine.



During a live broadcast I was following about the events of the war, I realised how small we are in the face of the circumstances happening in recent times. I was thinking that, in front of all this, we are so much like ants that we are almost easily subject to being trampled by "humans".

We went through the circumstances of covid for two years, a first risk of being "trampled", and now we find ourselves in the middle of a settling of scores between "powerful men". I can't believe you don't realise too that we are so small we can be crushed by "their" torments of control.

After all, what do we think we are?

We are like nothing, but we all are; we cannot help thinking that the great ones of this earth are too, and do you know why? Because we are all human beings, with the only difference that we live on different floors of the famous tower of Babel.

Read what is written in this verse from the first letter of Peter, chapter 1, verses 24-25:
As the scripture says, "All human beings are like the grass, and all their glory is like wild flowers. The grass dies, and the flowers fall off, but the word of the Lord remains for ever." This word is the Good News that was proclaimed to you.

How beautiful is this verse? Imagine that each of us is a blade of grass on this earth. Imagine also a world covered like a football pitch with that soft grass, wonderful to walk on. Now imagine that each of our successes is a flower sprouting on our head. A beautiful flowering earth, but how long can our success last? Grass too has its life cycle. The only question that comes to mind is why, if everything has a cycle, the Word of God is eternal.

In Ecclesiastes it is written that everything under this sky has already happened and there is nothing new; rather, things repeat themselves, the good and the wicked die and are forgotten, despite what they have done. Here is the answer: God has given everyone the chance to know an eternal truth. A truth visible in every past generation, certain in future generations, with the possibility of changing unpleasant events, but with the awareness that to this day, even in this present age, no one has ever managed it.

No man on this earth has ever managed to change the fate of a country, of a nation, or even of his own neighbourhood, not to mention his own apartment building, and I would even say his own landing, let alone his own home.

If something great is transformed from something small, then we should first understand what is inside us and then be an example to others. In Qoheleth it is indeed pointed out that our life is vanity. The only thing that has always guided us in the right direction has been God. Every promise of His has been fulfilled and is still valid, and has not yet been disproved by past or present events, and I believe it will never be disproved in the future either.

The reality is that we do not manage to know the true meaning of this life so as to live it at its best, as He showed us, but we keep fighting against ourselves and against the human race, thinking we can achieve every kind of well-being. We almost demand that myriads of flowers sprout on our heads, but let me tell you another secret: the Lord is the God of gold and silver, and do you know what I mean? That it is enough to hold on to Him to have what all men chase after. Everyone would have in abundance, and that is not my promise but, once again, His.

Having read and reread the books of the Bible over and over, I realise that the human race has always preferred toil to the freedom of enjoying life. Many men like me try to chase or discover freedom, but in the end all we are given to do is enjoy this flowering of our life. Do we have obligations? No, only respect and the direction God has given us to return to Him, aware of having lived in a way that pleases God.

The more I look around, the more I realise that none of this exists. Everyone would like to change I don't know what; many others want to change house, car, wife, clothes, and many more want to change life, job and so on. I have not yet met anyone who wants to live this life in a way that pleases God. Actually, maybe lately there are some I see enjoying life, and they are those camper-van people who have decided to be digital nomads. Of course, though, if you ask them whether they are doing it to please God, I don't even want to imagine the answers I would hear from their mouths.

Why is it so hard to reset everything? Am I the only one asking these questions? Am I the only one who thinks that resetting everything is like having different software that lets me do things the way I like, but then I stop again and think that I would need to know the programming language, and I fall into another panic? In reality, going back to Qoheleth, or Ecclesiastes, or Solomon, whatever you prefer to call him, one thing is clear, and you can discover it in the last verses of the last chapter of his book: the words of the wise are like goads, collected sayings are like firmly fixed nails; there is no end to the writing of books, and too much study will wear you out. After all this, there is only one thing that matters: have reverence for God and obey His commands. This is what everyone was created for, because in the end God will judge everything we do, good or bad, even things done in secret.

Have a good rest of your day.
