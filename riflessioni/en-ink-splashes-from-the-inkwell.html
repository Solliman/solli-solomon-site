13 May 2022 · Written by Solli Solomon

Ink splashes from the inkwell


When I started writing this article I had an explosion of things in my head, but there were so many that, to explain them well and quickly in a short time, the title came to mind first.

Once upon a time you had to dip the pen (literally a feather pen) into the inkwell to draw ink and start writing, and the speed my thoughts would have given my hand would probably have splashed ink everywhere.

It happens to us when we speak too: very often we have so many things to say that we even skip the words we need to pronounce. "I'm... to pick you... for a ride".

How hard do our thoughts work?

I don't know where we stand scientifically, but I believe that over the course of a day that little voice in our head sums up situations for us far more often than our mouth actually expresses our thoughts.

It is probably that very little voice, the one letting you read this article in silence, that greatly influences our speech and our being. It happens to me too, many times, to be there calculating, weighing, planning, and with that little voice I influence my own mood as well.

I would like to express what I feel right now, but that same wish to be right won't let me do better, so as not to hurt whoever is reading. Sometimes you should set the truth free, but you can't always argue with whoever said: you have to reflect before you think.

I was looking at one of the many "stories" posted on various social networks, and one in particular made me laugh. It said: when a person has a certain temperament you have to understand them, you have to be understanding.

I stopped to reflect and immediately considered the opposite situation, that is, when we rebel against the temperament of that person we are supposed to be understanding with. I am sure we would immediately be pointed at as the bad ones and certainly not excused for our attitude. The moral seems to be that someone who has always had a terrible temper must be understood, while someone who has always been understanding cannot be the opposite, and so is not accepted in the "tired and no longer understanding" version.

We are pointed at as bad examples, and we always confirm our humanity.

For years I was a bad example, and maybe I was accepted, sometimes praised by others and even respected, if not considered, no less, excellent. I think that if I had carried on I would have become someone important hahaha (yes, I'm laughing, even if your little voice is laughing like a robot ha ha ha). When I decided to turn my life around, I did it by making a healthy principle my own, and it did me a great deal of good, mainly to myself. With others, on the other hand, it only brought distance. Again scientifically, I don't know what the statistics on this are, but I believe that being reserved, calm, serene, happy, positive in the right sense, prudent, a good counsellor, clear, honest, not vulgar and so on tends to drive people away.

The world is afraid of right things. The world pushes away those who want to do good in the true sense of the word. The world looks kindly on those who do good, but it is miles away from the world of those who do good.

I discovered all this after deciding to turn my life around. Many turned it into a religious matter, others into a desire for different choices, but the moral is finding yourself alone. I ask myself: so all the world's desire for right things is actually falseness? If I apply to my life a truth I believe is right, which I also apply towards others, and which does not require the people I spend time with to change their lives, then why are people frightened? Maybe because it's more "cool" to add a vulgar word to every sentence, to use improper and equally vulgar terms, or to hug everyone without distinction of sex or respect for others, because otherwise it isn't "love" or caring for each other?

I truly can't make sense of all this, I really can't understand it, and yet I don't understand those who go to church (whatever the religion) and behave like "saints" for as long as they stay in church, only to walk out and betray all the sacraments with despicable words, deeds and missions.

It saddens me to notice how people who have chosen to improve their lives present a very subdued image on social media, and I have also seen proof of some people changing back, who started posting their former life on social media again, almost as if declaring regret for the time lost living in a restrained way.

Maybe I am so used to change that it doesn't frighten me. If before I had so many people around me and now none, it doesn't frighten me to know there are people who want to keep their distance.


I made one right choice in my life and I wouldn't change it for anything: I chose Christ. And although because of Him I lost a good part of the people I knew, I also lost the esteem of acquaintances because of something good, I lost the consideration of others who see me as a religious type; I don't think I have taken anything from anyone, but I have gained in terms of freedom.

A world that follows a current of continuous daily war, made of truces with night-time entertainment, economic interest and good positions in high society: why should I care, if in the end I know that everything has its time.

Yes, I make music, I play, I produce, I write, so you would naturally judge that I too do it to make myself known, but the purpose of my writing first of all, and of everything else that follows, is to reach those who are in need or who are looking for a truth. Being able to reach so many people in so little time is a positive thing.

Many are full of experiences, and these many stop there. Experiences are, as many have already explained at length, the sum of the mistakes made in one's life. I confirm this and add that experiences that stay stuck in themselves give little awareness, as well as sadness, depression, discouragement and so on. Only the next step can give life.

I have built up a lot of experience in my life by managing to make many mistakes, and I am so good at it that even today, although I am still trying to improve, I build up experience with daily mistakes, which says a lot about my perfection. This explains why I came to write, to produce, in short to do what I have the gift to do: to bring those who are tired of experiences and now depressed by life to hope.

Did you know that hope is the step above experience?

Whoever climbs this step deals, like me, with choices that are mainly personal and knowingly lonely; oh yes, because then there will be someone who will set you aside, discard you, push you away or see you as a burden.

If hope is like rushing to a shop to find the shoes still on sale, the ones I pointed out to you in the chat, then fine. If hope is that thing you begin to treat as certain, not yet visible but which you believe can be possible, then it is the awareness that you will see its real proof. For this last statement, then, I am a fool, because I believe more in this version of hope for my life.

That's how this world is: it considers good taste crazy and condemns it; it exalts the foolish, considering them heroes of salvation.

With all this, what could I expect? That you follow my advice? That you listen to me? That you might save yourself from the pressure of situations or conditions in your life? That you still want to read our blog? That you want to know Christ? That you put yourself on the line to try changing your ways of doing or being? That you might have a hope different from finding a discount in that shop?

Mocked, beaten, spat on and so on and so on, only afterwards was He recognised for what He IS; I hope you understood who I am referring to.

Could I ever expect you to accept today everything I have written and advised? But I want to tell you a secret: you have from today until the last day of your life to do it.
