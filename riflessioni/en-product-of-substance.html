Product of substance

One late summer evening, after spending a wonderful day in a little cove, we went to the home of a very dear friend.

Dear because of the substance of the person: one of those friends with whom you share no particular hobbies but with whom you communicate well, someone who listens with interest and curiosity and answers with care and maturity.


Skipping the preamble, which in itself is part of the substance this piece is about, I want to dwell on what happened at this friend's house.

He had always told me about his passion, but he had never let me taste what he calls "ciucculat" (chocolate).

He began to cut some guanciale; from the cut and the aroma you could already taste its flavour. He separated the eggs from the whites, using 3 yolks out of 4. The pasta water was already boiling. Everything fitted together like a well-oiled mechanism, able to work at its best.

All the ingredients were ready to be channelled into a single substance, potentially stitched together like a work of art. While we watched the final product come together, an almost obsessive collaboration between husband and wife played out, one of those scenes you see in the greatest restaurants between chef and assistant, a real delight to see a couple's unity even in this culinary interlude.

I had always believed that pasta, freshly drained, should be mixed straight away with the ingredients that give it flavour, but that evening my theory was taken apart. I noticed how the real success of a dish lay precisely in the right waiting time, so that everything could come together in the right context, in one shared space created especially for this meeting that would cause so much amazement.

Needless to say, if I have told you all this it is simply because the final result was far beyond my expectations. I was looking forward to a dish that would surely be good, a typical plate of pasta shared among friends, made with excellent cooking, but never, ever would I have expected to taste such an exceptional dish. Calling it exceptional is underselling it. You expect gourmet restaurants to offer you something like that, but never a simple guy who puts so much passion into what he says he does well. He says he does it well precisely because his experience has led him to perfect himself and to be sure of what he knows he can let you taste.

If the idea is not clear and you are closer to the world of music, then let me offer you a way to understand what I mean.

Do you know what a soundcheck is?

You know those hours before a concert when all the musicians play their instruments one by one so that the engineer can equalise each channel and then let them be heard "together" with the right final sound, allowing the evening to be a great success and, above all, the band to triumph?

Well, that dish was a great night at a concert of your favourite singer, hearing the sounds clean, clear and detailed, satisfied with what you had chosen.
That evening, for the first time in my life, I tasted a dish with defined flavours, ingredients that, although mixed together, stayed distinct on the palate, giving the dish an explosive taste.
I could spend hours writing about the fantastic feeling that dish, and not only the dish, stirred in me.

In the end I simply understood. Not because I am always there linking things to God, but I was truly inspired to understand that the conditions of life are exactly like this: they intersect, they meet again, they blend while remaining themselves, they burst into something good always and only if you know how to wait for the right time.
The rush to reach your own results, believing them to be good, is not equal to the right results that come from knowing what you want.

That is exactly why, by opening your eyes to the world, you discover things that go beyond a simple evening among friends: a real lesson given unknowingly by friends who do things the right way.

So often we hide behind many truths pulled out of the Bible and copied ad hoc to convince others of something that surely grows in a substantial and natural way even without knowing Christ. Here too there is a biblical lesson: the Holy Spirit also guides those who are unaware and do not know He is there to make Himself known.

That evening was God's answer to the events a couple goes through without knowing they are going through them, wrapped up in what the "world" offers, which carries you away and traps you.

God is omnipresent, and that does not mean He does not guide the unaware; on the contrary, He waits precisely for them to recognise that He is there, always has been, forever, for a new life full of many wonders.

Dedicated to you, dedicated to a friend.
