Happy Married!


We are a couple and we are ONE thing.
We overcome difficulties together, we smile but we also cry, we learn to love each other's  flaws and to give VALUE TO STRENGTHS, because strengths seem taken for granted and the flaws can often easily  be laid over them, and instead of strengthening the relationship they destroy it.

Instead, we are called to tear down the walls of silence, the inappropriate clashes that leave room for insults, prejudice and attempts to overpower the other;
we examine ourselves by asking God for help, and this  leads us to discover Love even more deeply and to experience it between us through FORGIVENESS and understanding, doing a RESET on whatever harmful thing would like to take root in and around us.
IS THIS the secret of a healthy marriage?

Pentidattilo

Yes! This is exactly it!
Let me dot one more "i":
Just as you share lunch AND dinner, also share the moments when you pray for each other; go for walks together, travel, be affectionate and kind outside the bedroom too. I'm talking to women as well.
