It seemed, but...


As far as I can remember, it has never been easy. From growing up, to first approaches, to exams, to new things, to suffering, to the journey.

On the straight stretches, where I could see far ahead, I was always afraid that something might jump out in front of me, and this forced me to be afraid, with the result that I went slowly.

When did I actually meet danger? On a bend, at a crossroads, on a broken road. They were the moments when I was relaxed, going at a moderate speed and in tune with my vehicle.

The idea is clear: it is not how slow or fast you go, but the difficulty of meeting something else on your road.

The safety of your life is knowing yourself. Knowing yourself comes from analysing your mistakes and the experiences you draw from them. Knowing yourself is not fulfilling yourself in order to reach the fulfilment of others. Knowing yourself gives you the chance to take your experiences and share them with others, so that the weight is redistributed.

It all seemed absurd, but change comes from your own will to be something new. By changing our mind and our actions, everything we were is transformed into a new creature.

Nothing is difficult, everything is knowledge. What you face with knowledge sets you free. Everything starts from the will; there are no chains that can bind you, but it is not a fight against others, it is a fight against your own mind and your own body.
