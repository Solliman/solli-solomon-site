Out of Covid-19

Almost two years after it arrived, we thought Covid infections could be counted on the fingers of one hand, given that about 80% of the Italian population chose to get "vaccinated".

Let us tell you straight away that, thank God, we came out alive from this unexpected Covid adventure.
We had already meant to write an article about the evolution of what we have called the "manipulating machine", but today more than ever we have been motivated, precisely because we experienced the "mockery" first-hand.

By now I think the whole world is aware, not just us, of having been the victims of a plan beyond all human imagination, a plan aimed at bringing humanity under a "21st-century yoke of slavery", in our view.

Death and the escape plan

What does man fear?
Death!
A well-studied plan to instil fear, a plan that would keep people imprisoned in their own homes, changing their habits, forcing them to face personal and family shortcomings, causing mostly more frustration than solutions. A plan that would paralyse almost everything economically, a plan in which the only escape route required being willing to accept any "thing" just to get back to normal, if we can call it that.

About the vaccine...

About two years after the pandemic began, we too had to deal with this virus about a month ago. First of all we wanted to make clear that, as required by law, we always wore a mask indoors and outdoors, certainly doing without it where it was NOT needed.

Despite all due care, the "mockery" arrives. Meanwhile, around us, people of all ages are sure the vaccine can never betray them, in some cases making those who don't have it, like the two of us, feel at fault or in danger. Hindsight confirms to us the unreliability of these serums (besides the testimonies of doctors and others), since the virus was transmitted by someone who had already completed the 2nd round of vaccination. The same vaccinated person had all the symptoms of a Covid infection, just like an unvaccinated one. What I have just described is not the result of guesswork but absolute certainty, reality.

Signs of weakness, tiredness and in the following days a slight fever, aching bones, cough, loss of taste and smell: these were the symptoms of the infection.
The treatment with antibiotics for six days and a syrup gave excellent results, taking away every worry, but the restriction of the following 3 weeks to test negative with a molecular swab was the most annoying part.

No panic

We wanted to share our thoughts because we're tired of hearing that the vaccine is a prevention against Covid-19.
By now very many people keep confirming that it's just a serum without any guarantee.
Today we want to confirm it too, not because we're experts on the subject but because we saw first-hand that we were infected by someone who had two doses of vaccine.
We don't want to leave you with fears, but to help you be aware that the serum doesn't give You superpowers or guarantee immunity, but, more importantly, there is a fairly effective Cure.

ADVICE:
– Don't underestimate the infection;
– follow the treatment;
– drink a lot;
– don't eat junk food;
– if you can, walk, do physical activity, taking care not to sweat too much;
– above all, No Panic!

We are sure of this: in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.

Romans 8:28

This is one of the verses that, during this time of quarantine, encouraged us to believe we would be free of the infection, free to act according to the purpose God still has for our life today.

We hope this experience too can help those who live in terror of being infected to believe in a complete healing , and partly expose what the system has so far wanted us to believe, bringing society into chaos: discouraging it with false news, imprisoning it in fear and "gently" threatening it with a Green Pass.
