In the Kitchen with Grandma Maria


Discover the Irpinian recipe
for "pizza with herbs" by
"Grandma Maria"

"Grandma Maria" as a little girl used to do the cleaning, obeying what her mother asked her, but her wish was to help her in the kitchen.

For many years she longed to get her hands into that kitchen; she had "stolen with her eyes" every step of her beloved pizza with herbs, and "today", proudly, she shows us how much love she puts into making this fantastic Irpinian recipe of pizza with wild herbs, carefully picked by her.

"Today" it is a great Redemption for her to be able to devote her time to enriching young and not-so-young people through her talent and her life experience.

Thank you, "Grandma Maria"
