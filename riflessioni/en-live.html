LIVE!


I will no longer hide my wings!
Why not remember again the pleasure of spreading them and beating them in the air?
For a long time they cried out to fly, but something managed to send the sound back. I walked, I ran in vain,
breathless and desperate for not having reached goals that others had set for me. Suddenly a Sound wrapped me completely, cleaning my wings stained with black. "LIVE!" it told me.

You cannot live someone else's life; each of our lives is unique, an extraordinary GIFT that has been brought into the world.

Maybe you can't grasp this idea because you have unconsciously absorbed the wrong standards, and you don't think you are good enough to make your contribution to this society.

Do you feel incapable, insignificant, do you believe you have no value? OK! But now it's time to put an end to it!
IF SOMEONE ALLOWED YOU TO BE HERE, THEY THOUGHT to equip you with something unique, and only you are able to do what you have been called to live for.
LIVE!  your  life.

Jaffa Port
