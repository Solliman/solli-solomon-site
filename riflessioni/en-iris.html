IRIS


I don't want to give you my interpretation of what Biagio Antonacci wanted to communicate with one of his songs, "Iris", but something else entirely.

A few days ago a flower called Iris led me to a reflection. It wasn't a talking flower, don't misunderstand me; I just mean that when you're ready to receive a lesson, anything around you can spark such curiosity that it leads You to a new milestone of knowledge and maturity. Has it never happened to you?

Is it perhaps a Lily

I always thought that the symbol on the coat of arms of Florence was a Lily. I never looked into it or searched for an answer on Google myself, partly because, by "hearsay", others had also identified that flower as a Lily. I found myself at home with a bunch of flowers given to me by my mother-in-law (a great green thumb), a blue-violet colour with a long green stem; they cheered my heart and, as I mentioned, drew me to get to know them a bit better. Around the corner the turning point was waiting for me: what I had always wrongly considered a Lily on the coat of arms of Florence was actually an Iris.

Between Fakes and Appearances

This made me reflect on how many times I thought I knew facts about people based on personal assumptions or because I was influenced by other people's opinions. They say that knowledge sets you FREE. But how many times, instead of APPLYING myself to searching for the Truth, did I prefer to look at appearances or listen to "word on the street" and chain myself up with my own hands?


Questions

Why weigh yourself down with JUDGEMENTS about other PEOPLE's lives and TRAIN YOUR TONGUE with RUMOURS and GOSSIP? Why not desire to TRULY KNOW yourself, so as to be FREE TO BE ABLE TO TRULY HELP OTHERS??

If you're reading this, you're perhaps thinking you've never opened your mouth out of turn, right? It's always like that! Have you ever thought of looking the targeted person in the face and telling them what you really think of them? Maybe they could get hurt? But you'll do it anyway, stabbing them in the back. Better a "face to face" duel that tastes of honesty!

It's easier to justify our sharp tongues than to stop for a moment and reflect on how much judgement we "vomit" onto others every day, spreading an identity that doesn't belong to them, isn't it?

Did you know that judging others is like the weeds that grow in a field of wheat, which, once the wheat has grown, are burned? So why waste breath and time on something that brings no benefit to your life?

How many chances you have

I wrote all this because, analysing myself, I understand how badly I handled some situations: fruitless conversations that should have been cut short, remarks about how others manage their lives, etc. etc.

I may sound repetitive, but comparing what I live with what I read in the Bible, I understand how much "crookedness" lives in me and how much I still have to grow in His love. Just as Christ gave me the chance to reassess everything in my life, helping me recognise my mistakes and be forgiven EVERY single day, the same chance is given to others.

So it's good not to give in to judgement, because by respecting other people's weaknesses you will respect yourself, knowing that you too are fighting the same weaknesses, maybe different ones, but weaknesses all the same.
