La Musica Cambia - musical single


We want to share with you, dear reader friend, one of the many projects that we are seeing come to fruition: the first concerns "Music changes", the title of our first single.

It's a song born from words and music is the frame. It is certainly an inspired song and not written to put together a text to produce music. In Italy there was a lack of a positive message in electronic music. There are many Italian Christian songwriters and singers of the Pop, Rock, Rap, HipHop, Trap etc. genres. which certainly produced rhythmic and danceable songs but what was missing was the electronic genre developed with a specific dance concept, with a positive message. The text is very simple, concrete, with a repetition that involves you in the rhythm and above all in the singing. I define it as a song that you dance and sing with your arms stretched upwards as if you want to recover from something that has now sucked you in. It is a declaration of freedom and also of desire for cchange.

A song that before involving you in a dance wants to lead you to a reflection, the ''key'' is evident at the end of the text: "A Source of Living Water" that regenerates, vivifies, brings change. Just as a man's organism needs water to regenerate, the spiritual part must also be nourished to be in balance. The source of Living Water par excellence?

Jesus.

Are you wondering why I chose this cover, I'll explain it to you below. 

The cover represents Christ's first miracle. They are demijohns with water inside to indicate that there is something still to change.

Il miracolo è operato da Gesù, noi siamo coloro che portano i contenitori a Lui per operare una trasformazione.

It has been very uplifting for us to work on this project: as a couple, growing in working closely together; as a relationship, strengthening for having worked closely with God. Now we just have to get excited thinking that we have succeeded and finally see our first project come to fruition. PS: Two more songs coming out soon.

You will find the song on most of the currently most popular music stores and above all you can use it in your Instagram, Facebook and Tik Tok stories. Thank you in advance for listening and sharing.
