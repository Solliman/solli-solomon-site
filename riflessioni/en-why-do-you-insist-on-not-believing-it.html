Why do you insist on not believing it!


Hey! Yes, you! Do you know you weren't made in a factory?

Why do you keep saying: "I feel different; I feel misunderstood; I feel like the black sheep; and blah blah blah...


You are unique, just like your fingerprint. Perhaps unknown and ignored in the eyes of many? It doesn't matter, because this DOES NOT DIMINISH THE VALUE YOU HAVE.

View over the sea – Palmi

Just like the oxygen you can't see, but which is there and is essential , so is the GIFT that you are (MAYBE YOU STILL HAVE TO REALISE IT):

It has impact, it brings balance, it digs beyond appearances, it fights, it accepts confrontation, it uproots itself from clay soil, it builds on rock, it paints on snow, it can draw aside curtains of rain, it sees seabeds in the mountains, and it GLIMPSES STRUGGLES OF REVOLUTION AMONG BARE BRANCHES... where the NEW will sprout!
