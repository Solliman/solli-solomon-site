001 — Biography

Playing the truth

Solli Solomon is an Italian producer where sound becomes prayer and rhythm becomes revelation. Moving between techno, experimental and contemplative electronics, each release is a journey inward, each beat a question about what lies beyond.

Productions carry a rare sincerity: rooted in spiritual depth, they don't just fill a room, they open one. Whether born from the Word or pure silence, every production carries a rare sincerity in a world of noise.

Collaborations have enriched the journey: with Star Bstreet — his wife and voice of numerous tracks — he has created some of the most spiritually intense tracks in his discography. He collaborated with Francesco Fontanella for the APOC 13 remix, with Radio Gatto for an early remix, and recently signed a remix for the album Ethos by Otus Medi.
