001 — Biography

Playing the truth

Solli Solomon is a pioneering Italian producer where sound becomes prayer and rhythm becomes revelation. His artistic path is deeply rooted in Christian techno and contemplative, experimental electronic music — a journey where every frequency is a spiritual intention, and every beat a search for truth in Christ.

Born from a desire to bring praise and meditation to club culture and contemporary electronic music settings, Solli Solomon crafts sonic landscapes designed for prayer, inner silence, and spiritual sharing. His music is conceived as an electronic liturgy that opens up a sacred space through the intentional use of synthesizers and rhythm.

Collaborations have enriched this journey of faith and sound: with Star Bstreet — his wife and voice of numerous tracks — he has created some of the most spiritually intense tracks in his discography. He has also collaborated with Francesco Fontanella for the APOC 13 remix, with Radio Gatto for an early remix, and recently signed a remix for the album Ethos by Otus Medi.
