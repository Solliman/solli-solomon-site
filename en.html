001 — Biography

Play the truth

Solli Solomon is an Italian producer where sound becomes prayer and rhythm becomes revelation. Moving between techno, experimental and contemplative electronics, each release is a journey inward, each beat a question about what lies beyond.

Rooted in spiritual depth and creative intuition, he builds soundscapes that don't just fill a room — they open it. Whether born from the Word or from pure silence, each production carries a rare sincerity in a world of noise.

Collaborations have enriched the journey: with Star Bstreet — his wife and vocalist on numerous tracks — he has created some of the most spiritually intense tracks of his discography. He collaborated with Francesco Fontanella for the remix of APOC 13, with Radio Gatto for a first remix, and recently signed a remix for the Ethos album by Otus Medi.
